Governance Glossary

The terminology is converging. The implementations are not.

On LinkedIn, I see a lot of GPT-inspired posts claiming "a lot of the conversation around AI governance talks about X, when they should be talking about Y" GÇö where Y is a vaguely different capability that most systems actually already have. This page exists to prove the opposite point: the USPs people are selling are more universal than they think. It is structured by function through the SECS algebra, with industry equivalents mapped alongside. It is not a selling tool. It is a reference for anyone working in the space.
